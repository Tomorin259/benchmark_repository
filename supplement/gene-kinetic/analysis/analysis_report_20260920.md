# Gene-kinetic gene/cell edge analysis (2026-09-20)

This report is generated by `gene_kinetic_analysis.py` from the 15 phase-portrait manifests, the fixed-reference-30 gene edge table, the Diaph3 supplement, and the two new extended cell-level tables. The candidate set is small and purposeful; it is descriptive evidence, not an overall benchmark.

## Source pairing

- 15 manifests produce 28 displayed gene edges and 252 gene-method-edge rows.
- Valid gene-CBDir values: 237/252.
- Exact cell pairing for both native CBDir and `batc_candidate_source_mean`: 243/252 rows.
- Source audit/path agreement: 135/135 case×method mappings.
- The phase contract names the spliced velocity layer `velocity`; 30 audit mappings expose method-specific aliases (`velocity_reconstructed` or `velocity_gv`) and are recorded explicitly rather than silently renamed.
- DeepVelo, UniTVelo, and veloVI use gene-matched H5AD outputs. The other six methods use canonical rerun outputs. The full mapping is in `source_mapping_audit.csv`.

## Primary-edge association after gene aggregation

To avoid pseudo-replication, genes sharing the same dataset/source/target/method are first averaged into one edge-method row. The values below compare gene-CBDir with the corresponding cell-level score; they do not combine directionality with consistency metrics.

- cbdir: n=62, Pearson=0.259, Spearman=0.129, sign match=0.774.
- batc_candidate_source_mean: n=62, Pearson=0.248, Spearman=0.131, sign match=0.742.

Within-edge-centered correlations are reported in `correlation_summary.csv`. They use method-centered residuals and should be treated as exploratory when the number of independent edges is small.
Method-specific correlations across edges are in `correlation_by_method.csv`; most have fewer than ten independent edges and are therefore exploratory.
The per-edge nine-method correlations used below are recorded in `edge_correlation_summary.csv`.

## Concrete edge-level findings (descriptive)

- Gastrulation Erythroid2→Erythroid3: the seven genes are aggregated by method before comparing the nine methods. Gene vs cell-CBDir has Spearman 0.583, Pearson 0.859; gene vs BATC candidate has Spearman 0.583, Pearson 0.803. All 9/9 method signs agree for both metrics. Typical positive methods are TIvelo (gene=0.573, cell-CBDir=0.827, BATC-candidate=0.954) and cellDancer (gene=0.329, cell-CBDir=0.572, BATC-candidate=0.646); typical negative methods are DeepVelo_scv (gene=-0.380, cell-CBDir=-0.695, BATC-candidate=-0.800) and MultiVelo (gene=-0.475, cell-CBDir=-0.555, BATC-candidate=-0.225).
- capo G1→S: after aggregating the two genes, 8/9 methods have matching signs. The gene vs cell-CBDir association is Pearson 0.526/Spearman 0.317; for BATC candidate it is Pearson 0.677/Spearman 0.483. This is a moderate descriptive association from one edge and nine methods.
- Pancreas Pre-endocrine→Beta: three methods have negative gene scores (DeepVelo_scv, UniTVelo, scVelo_dyn), while all nine cell-CBDir scores are positive (0.566 to 0.790) and all nine BATC candidate means are positive (0.701 to 0.813). This is a clear direction-level decoupling on a single edge.
- Retina Neuroblast→RGC: two methods have negative gene scores (DeepVelo_scv, scVelo_dyn), while all nine cell-CBDir and BATC candidate scores are positive. The corresponding sign agreement is 7/9 for both metrics. This is another descriptive decoupling, not evidence that a gene direction is biologically unnecessary in general.
- Ncald ImmGranule1→ImmGranule2: cellDancer is an opposite-sign exception (cellDancer (gene=0.211, cell-CBDir=-0.483, BATC-candidate=-0.597)); its gene score is positive while both cell scores are negative. This is a concrete example against treating a positive gene score as sufficient for a positive cell-direction score.
- After gene aggregation, primary-edge sign agreement is 0.774 for cell-CBDir and 0.742 for BATC candidate, versus 0.585 and 0.604 on secondary/control edges. The primary/control difference is descriptive and based on only seven and six independent displayed edges, respectively.

## How to read BATC candidate values

`batc_candidate_source_mean` is the mean candidate cosine before per-source branch maximization. `branch_winner_share` is the fraction of source cells for which the edge won among the candidate targets in the full extended table. A high candidate mean does not imply a high overall BATC or a high winner share. Branching-source diagnostics are in `branching_winner_share.csv`.

## Supported conclusions

- The exact paired data can test descriptive association between gene-CBDir and native cell CBDir or pre-max candidate means on these fixed edges.
- Method rankings and sign agreement vary by edge; the per-edge rank and sign tables expose this heterogeneity.
- Branching edges can separate candidate directional similarity from branch selection, because winner share is reported independently.

## Conclusions not supported

- These rows cannot establish that recovering a gene direction causes better cell direction, or that either metric identifies a mechanism.
- The 15 manifest cases cannot support overall method ranking or population-level benchmark claims.
- BATC candidate means and winner shares cannot be described as the final BATC score or as a biological probability of the target fate.
- Correlations across genes sharing one edge must not be counted as independent evidence; all main correlation tables aggregate them first.

## Minimal next experiment

Pre-register a small matched control panel for each biological process (the same source/target edges with several non-prior genes), rerun the exact native CBDir and candidate-mean/winner-share pipeline on the same fixed neighbors, and test whether the prior genes add signal beyond edge- and method-specific baselines. For a mechanistic claim, perturb one candidate gene and measure the corresponding transition plus an orthogonal lineage or state readout.
