# Exploratory gene–cell edge analysis

This exploratory report is generated by `gene_kinetic_analysis.py` from the 15 phase-portrait manifests, the current reference-30 Gene edge table, the Diaph3 supplement, and the retained canonical/gene-matched Cell edge tables. It compares gene- and cell-level directionality on the displayed transitions. Gene scores include veloVI's coordinate-unit restoration; the phase portraits retain native coordinates. Input and output identities are in `provenance.json`.

## Source pairing

- 15 manifests produce 28 displayed gene edges and 252 gene-method-edge rows.
- Valid gene-CBDir values: 237/252.
- Exact cell pairing for both native CBDir and `batc_candidate_source_mean`: 243/252 rows.
- Source audit/path agreement: 135/135 case×method mappings.
- The phase contract names the spliced velocity layer `velocity`; the mapping table records 30 method-specific aliases (`velocity_reconstructed` or `velocity_gv`).
- DeepVelo, UniTVelo, and veloVI use gene-matched H5AD outputs. The other six methods use the retained canonical Cell outputs. The full mapping is in `source_mapping_audit.csv`.

## Primary-edge association after gene aggregation

To avoid pseudo-replication, genes sharing the same dataset/source/target/method are first averaged into one edge-method row. The values below compare gene-CBDir with the corresponding cell-level directionality score.

- cbdir: n=62, Pearson=0.257, Spearman=0.111, sign match=0.758.
- batc_candidate_source_mean: n=62, Pearson=0.245, Spearman=0.116, sign match=0.726.

Within-edge-centered correlations are reported in `correlation_summary.csv`. They use method-centered residuals and should be treated as exploratory when the number of independent edges is small.
Method-specific correlations across edges are in `correlation_by_method.csv`; most have fewer than ten independent edges and are therefore exploratory.
The per-edge nine-method correlations used below are recorded in `edge_correlation_summary.csv`.

## Concrete edge-level findings (descriptive)

- Gastrulation Erythroid2→Erythroid3: the seven genes are aggregated by method before comparing the nine methods. Gene vs cell-CBDir has Spearman 0.583, Pearson 0.853; gene vs BATC candidate has Spearman 0.583, Pearson 0.796. Sign agreement is 8/9 for cell-CBDir and 8/9 for BATC candidate. Positive examples are TIvelo (gene=0.573, cell-CBDir=0.827, BATC-candidate=0.954) and cellDancer (gene=0.329, cell-CBDir=0.572, BATC-candidate=0.646); negative examples are DeepVelo_scv (gene=-0.380, cell-CBDir=-0.695, BATC-candidate=-0.800) and MultiVelo (gene=-0.475, cell-CBDir=-0.555, BATC-candidate=-0.225).
- capo G1→S: after aggregating the two genes, cell-CBDir sign agreement is 8/9. The gene vs cell-CBDir association is Pearson 0.508/Spearman 0.283; for BATC candidate it is Pearson 0.662/Spearman 0.450. These are descriptive associations from one edge and nine methods.
- Pancreas Pre-endocrine→Beta: negative Gene scores occur for DeepVelo_scv, UniTVelo, scVelo_dyn. Cell-CBDir spans 0.566 to 0.790; BATC candidate means span 0.701 to 0.813. This is direction-level decoupling on the displayed edge.
- Retina Neuroblast→RGC: negative Gene scores occur for DeepVelo_scv, scVelo_dyn. Sign agreement is 7/9 for cell-CBDir and 7/9 for BATC candidate.
- Ncald ImmGranule1→ImmGranule2: cellDancer (gene=0.211, cell-CBDir=-0.483, BATC-candidate=-0.597) has a positive Gene score and negative Cell scores on the displayed transition.
- After gene aggregation, primary-edge sign agreement is 0.758 for cell-CBDir and 0.726 for BATC candidate, versus 0.604 and 0.585 on secondary/control edges. The primary/control difference is descriptive and based on only seven and six independent displayed edges, respectively.

## How to read BATC candidate values

`batc_candidate_source_mean` is the mean candidate cosine before per-source branch maximization. `branch_winner_share` is the fraction of source cells for which the edge won among the candidate targets in the full extended table. Branching-source diagnostics are in `branching_winner_share.csv`.

## Cross-level findings

- Paired data summarize associations between gene-CBDir and native cell CBDir or pre-max candidate means on the predefined edges.
- Method rankings and sign agreement vary by edge; the per-edge rank and sign tables expose this heterogeneity.
- Branching edges can separate candidate directional similarity from branch selection, because winner share is reported independently.
